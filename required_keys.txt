-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  THE INTERVIEWER AND THE STRATEGIST (spec §6.2, §6.5)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  NOT READ YET. The turn loop lands on day 2-3 of the build order; this entry
  describes what the key will buy so it is in keys.txt before then.

  GROQ_API_KEYS     One or more Groq keys, comma-separated.

                    With it: questions and probes are phrased for the moment,
                    and ProbeStrategist reads each answer (one structured call
                    per turn, `classification` tier).

                    Without it: questions and probes are the items' own stems
                    and follow-up templates, verbatim, and the strategist falls
                    back to spec §10's rule — deepen once, then move on. The
                    interview still runs; it is less adaptive, and the report
                    says so.

                    A POOL, not a key. Groq rate-limits per key, and several
                    concurrent sessions each make two calls per turn.

                    Get one at https://console.groq.com/keys

  Which MODEL each tier calls is not configured here — `ai_core.models` is the
  platform roster. Override without editing it:

  AI_MODEL_GENERATION       the interviewer's phrasing, platform-wide
  AI_MODEL_CLASSIFICATION   the strategist, platform-wide

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  M04 SERVICE ROUTES (spec §6.1)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  M04_SERVICE_SECRET
                    What M04's `require_service` checks, and what M06 sends as
                    X-Service-Secret for the pinned profile summary and the
                    posterior. Must match M04's keys.txt.

                    Without it M04's service routes are OPEN on a dev machine
                    (M04 warns once at startup), so nothing breaks locally.

                    M13 reads a bare SERVICE_SECRET instead; M06 accepts that
                    name as a fallback so one line can serve both.
